## Why

The operator played a full descent and reported eleven things (verbatim, typos
fixed):

1. There is no need for the minimap.
2. When I win the room, I do not understand which room to take.
3. The Pyre Mother was always in the corner; she should move more.
4. Her corpse ended in the wall.
5. The boss bar could be bigger.
6. The blood doesn't feel like it is red.
7. When I am in the room, all lights should be up all the time.
8. I won all three floors on the first try; all bosses felt quite similar.
   (The operator wrote "want"; read as "won". The operator then approved
   making ordinary enemies hit harder (D10), which is consistent with that.)
9. Spending pearls is a boring dialog; improve the UX ×10.
10. All rooms still feel quite similar.
11. I do not understand the weapon system; there should be a way to buy or
    unlock new weapons, and I should be able to choose.

**This playtest is the evidence plans 021 (Stage H), 022 (Stage G), 023
(Stage F) and 024 (Stage F) were waiting for.** Items 8 and 10 are not new
problems: they sit under stop rules that already tripped. This plan does not
re-tune difficulty on its own authority; Stage E records the playtest against
those open decisions and asks for them.

## Stages

Order is strict. A and B are pure fixes with no operator input. C is the
altar; it needs D8/D9. D and E need D10–D12. One stage per PR.

### Stage A — Quick fixes (D1, D4, D5, D7)

1. Remove the `.floor-map` widget from the HUD (markup and CSS, including the
   mobile overrides). Keep `.map-expanded` and the pause entry.
2. `canStand(cells, x, z, radius)`: callers pass `0.32 * look.scale`. Add
   `deathFall` and use it in `startDeath`.
3. Boss bar CSS per D5.
4. Blood `colorSpace`; capture a before/after with `npm run shots:compare`
   (SwiftShader) and record the splat's mean hue and chroma in
   `game/progress.md`.

Tests (each must fail with its bug planted — say which in the PR):
- Node: `deathFall` against a wall behind, a wall behind and in front, and a
  corner: the fallen footprint lies on floor. Plant: return the backwards
  direction unchanged.
- Node: `moveOnFloor` with scale 1.5 next to a wall keeps 0.48 clear. Plant:
  ignore scale.
- Browser: the HUD has no `.floor-map` element while playing, and Tab still
  opens the map. Plant: restore the widget.
- Browser: the Mother killed backed against a wall leaves a corpse whose
  snapshot bounding box is inside floor cells. Precondition: assert she stood
  within 0.6 of a wall at death.

Gates: typecheck, lint, `npm test`, the PR-gate browser run.

### Stage B — Doors that tell you, lights that stay on (D2, D6)

1. Count light sources per chamber over the seed corpus that the generator
   tests use; record the maximum. Stop rule: if a chamber needs more than 8 and
   thinning sconces would leave a wall with none, stop and report.
2. Pure `chamberLights(chamber)` (in `dungeon-radiance.ts` or a sibling, no
   Three.js): returns the ordered list of sources, so it can be tested in node.
   The world assigns the pool from it on chamber entry and fades over 0.3 s.
3. Door light, larger sigils with a floating label, the camera ease and the new
   clear notice (D2). Reduced motion turns off the ease and the bob.

Tests:
- Node: `chamberLights` returns only sources inside the chamber and at most 8,
  and includes every open door after a clear. Plant: nearest-to-knight
  selection across chambers.
- Browser: after a clear, the snapshot reports a light in each open door's
  tint, and every brazier in the chamber has a light assigned while the knight
  walks from one end to the other (observe what the scene placed, not the
  planner). Plant: assign by distance.
- Browser: the clear notice names the rewards behind the open doors.
- Frame budget: the existing budget scenario holds; the compile count does not
  rise. Record the number of lights on floor 1 next to progress.md's "Light
  cap" figure (9).

### Stage C — The altar hall as a shop (D8)

Pure rules (price, affordability, rank,
`canTry`) stay in `dungeon-meta.ts`; the hall wires them.

1. All racks visible; locked silhouettes, plaques, the stat card at the rack.
2. Try-before-buy: a locked arm can be wielded inside the hall only;
   `lockArm` refuses a locked arm at the way down (assert it in node).
3. Hold-to-buy with ring, chime, pearl burst and counter tick; upgrade shrines
   with rank notches.
4. Pearl counter in the hall only (minimal HUD: it is gone once the descent
   starts).
5. The panel becomes a pause-menu list.

Tests: node for hold duration, insufficient pearls, rank caps and "a tried
arm is not owned"; browser for "walking down with a tried, unbought arm starts
the run with the owned arm" (plant: lock whatever is in hand) and for a real
keyboard hold buying exactly once (plant: buy on keydown). Ask the operator to
play the hall before merging; record the verdict.

### Stage D — The Mother moves; then one boss at a time (D3, D11)

1. D3 first (no operator input needed beyond this plan): `repositionTarget` and

### Stage E — Harder rooms (D10)

1. Record this playtest in progress.md as the playtest evidence for plans
   021–024, and update their rows in `plans/README.md`.
2. Ordinary-enemy damage ×1.5 (one constant in the bestiary or combat rules,
   not per kind). Re-run `balance:check`, step per D10, and update
   `bands.json` from the measurements (renderer and date beside them).
3. Node test: an ordinary guard's swing deals the scaled damage and a boss's

### Stage F — Fuller rooms (D12 a, c; D9)

1. Props: pure `furnishRoom(room, theme, random)` in `dungeon-floor.ts` (or a
   sibling) returns typed props on free floor cells that keep every door, the
   spawn ring and a walkable path clear. Its prop roll comes from a salted
   stream; the existing layout bytes do not change.
2. Prop behaviour (break, blast, spikes, cover) as pure rules in
   `dungeon-hits.ts` / `dungeon-combat.ts`; the world only draws them.
3. New rewards *Boon*, *Pearls*, and the rare `arm` (D9).
4. Sim: `ordinaryDamagePerChamber` and fight length with props on and off.
   A prop that changes neither is decoration; say so.

Tests: node for "props never block a door or the path between doors" over the
generator's seed corpus (plant: skip the path check), blast hurting enemies
and the knight, spike telegraph length, and the `arm` reward's rarity and
"at most one per run" over many seeds (plant: drop the cap). Browser: a real
sword swing breaks an urn; the frame-budget scenario holds.

### Stage G — New enemy kinds (D12 b)

1. Measure first: the kinds met per floor over 30 default-bot runs; record
   the table. If a kind already in the roster almost never appears, raising
   its share in the pack mix is sub-stage G0 and costs no art.
2. Then one kind per sub-stage, in the order bomber, chanter, swarm, drowned
   (the drowned only on the flooded theme). Each follows the bestiary's
   checklist for a new kind (the header comment in `dungeon-bestiary.ts`):
   figure on an existing skeleton, palette row, cutaway, cause label,
   `npm run figures`, the draw-call ceiling, a pure decision rule with its node
   test, a `balance:check` run, and a browser wiring test.
3. Ask the operator to play each new kind before the next one starts.

## Stop rules

- A stage that needs more than the 8 pooled lights, or raises the compile
  count, stops and reports.
- A planted bug that survives a test means the test is weak; fix the test
  before the stage counts.
- Any threshold that has to be loosened to get green must first be shown not
  to be a real bug (see AGENTS.md).
- Stage E: if x1.5 (or the step toward x1.25) puts the weak bot above 20% escape or
  the skilled bot below 75%, stop and show the table before shipping.

## Not in scope

- A new renderer, bigger light pool, or post stack.
- Rewriting `dungeon-game.tsx` or a formatter pass.
